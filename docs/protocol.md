# Frozen v1 protocol

Version 1, 2026-11-19. Unit: image. Population: all valid images in the immutable release manifest, selected independently of proposals and reference corrections. Incidents concern omitted pedestrian boxes under the release policy. A seeded image and its verified duplicates are excluded from retrieval discovery credit, but not automatically from an independent audit. Unknown grouping metadata stays unknown; no encounter or drive claims without verified IDs.

Fixture generation seed 20261119; explicit fixture images are deterministic synthetic scenes. Fixture truth is machine-generated and kept separately from original annotations, proposals and audit review. Any fixture review event is marked `scripted_fixture`; no human pilot is claimed. Fixture demonstration is not an external accuracy result.

Coordinates are half-open original-pixel xyxy; IoU matching threshold 0.5 inclusive, deterministic ties by IDs. Ignore regions use intersection-over-candidate-area >= 0.5. ReChecked external evaluation freezes probability >= 0.8 and height >= 40 pixels; alternative settings are sensitivity analyses. The benchmark must reproduce upstream missed-versus-misfitting rules and explicitly report deviations and inaccessible inputs. Reference absence is unknown, not a confirmed negative. Evaluation requires independently defined eligible image population.

Retrieval: one adjudicated seed, exact cosine for available normalized crop embeddings; deterministic reciprocal rank fusion constant 60, equal signal weights, absent signal contributes zero, ties by image then proposal ID. One highest-ranked item per image; preserve overlap and provenance. No learned probabilities or prevalence from ranking. Detector confidence and crop similarity baselines remain separate. Development/final split is grouped by verified drive or duplicate group, never randomly by adjacent frame. Fixture split seed 20261119; group assignment is deterministic hash of seed and group ID, 80% development and 20% final; no claim of independence when grouping is unavailable. Review budgets 25, 50, 100 images; report available count when smaller. References remain private to scoring and never influence indexing or ranking.

Audit: prefreeze all valid release images, strata, fixed budget and seed 20261119 before decisions; merge undersized strata as needed to give every image positive inclusion probability. Sample uniformly without replacement within strata; probability n_h/N_h. Zero-proposal images remain eligible. Full-frame reviewers are blind to ranking and obtain new decisions even when queue reviewed. Estimate weighted image prevalence only for fully resolved draws; exact hypergeometric inversion with simultaneous Bonferroni 95% bounds and finite population treatment. Unresolved decisions yield zero/one sensitivity bounds and no completed point estimate. No optional stopping or inference to future drives. Exact small-population coverage and >=1000 seeded simulations are required before statistical validation claims.

Models are not required for the offline fixture. No model version, inference or human review is claimed until actually run and recorded. External data, GPU inference, independent human review and upstream benchmark reproduction remain explicit gates, not fixture substitutes.
